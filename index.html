01 About me

I am a Year-3 Computer Science student at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU). My coursework has taken me from the fundamentals of socket programming and protocol analysis to the engineering trade-offs behind large-scale distributed computation frameworks such as Hadoop and Spark, and on to practical machine-learning pipelines.

What excites me most is the moment a network trace turns into a story: a DNS answer, a TCP handshake, a TLS negotiation and an HTTP/2 stream each explain a small piece of why the web feels fast — or slow. This site itself is a small experiment in that spirit: hand-crafted, dependency-free, and served by GitHub Pages over HTTPS and HTTP/2.

Video streaming over TCP & UDP

A Python socket-programming project that streams video frames over both TCP and UDP, with receiver-side reassembly, sequence tracking and a Wireshark-based comparison of reliability, latency and throughput.

  • Loss/jitter measured from real captures

MapReduce & Spark word-count benchmark

The same corpus counted three ways — pure-Python MapReduce, Hadoop MapReduce and PySpark — then compared for implementation complexity, execution model and runtime at several data sizes.

End-to-end pipeline on PySpark: ingestion, EDA, feature engineering with transformers, model training, hyper-parameter selection and metric-based evaluation (accuracy, precision/recall, F1, AUC).
